- **TODO (0.1.1) — PR #27 review follow-ups (deferred from v0.1.0; user chose to ship as-is):**
  - ✅ **Done (current arc, `fix/download-resume-hardening`) — Codex P2: re-check cache before retrying
    a locked download.** The single-stream hf-hub fallback's lock-retry was folded into `fetch_one`,
    and the clean-layout + HF-cache fast paths were extracted into a `place_if_cached` helper that is
    re-checked **after each `LockAcquisition` backoff**: if the lock holder finishes during the sleep,
    the loser copies the now-present finalized blob into the clean layout and short-circuits instead of
    re-downloading or colliding on publish. The bounded backoff was also extended (per-attempt cap +
    more attempts → ~5 min total patience, vs the old ~20 s that could abandon a real multi-GB
    download), while the cache re-check exits the instant the holder is done. (`place_if_cached` unit
    tests cover the dest-present short-circuit and the miss path.) *Note:* this closes the PR #27
    Codex-P2 follow-up only; the separate `#46` row — orphaned **detached** chunk writers in the same
    single-stream fallback — stays open (it needs replacing hf-hub's high-level downloader).
