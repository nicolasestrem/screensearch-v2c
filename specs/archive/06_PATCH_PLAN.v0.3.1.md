| 22 | P1 | **0.3.1 PR2 (#64): the roadmap's fix premise is contradicted by release-build profiling.** `docs/0.3.1.md §1`/PR2 assumes the WebP *encode* lands synchronously on the vision hot path ("GPU utilization spikes per frame … signature of the encode step") and orders fixes accordingly (D5: async encode → cheaper encoder → format revert).
